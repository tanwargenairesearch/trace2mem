# Harbor capacity calculations (tool, 50% headroom)

Tool calculations: average 400 events/s yields 51,840,000,000 raw bytes/day (~48.3 GiB/day) needing 1 replica; peak 2400 events/s with 1.5x headroom needs 6 replicas (3600 events/s). Assumption excludes indexes, replicas, compression, metadata. Implication: 30-day raw retention (~1.45 TiB) is infeasible in PostgreSQL with 100 GiB free.
