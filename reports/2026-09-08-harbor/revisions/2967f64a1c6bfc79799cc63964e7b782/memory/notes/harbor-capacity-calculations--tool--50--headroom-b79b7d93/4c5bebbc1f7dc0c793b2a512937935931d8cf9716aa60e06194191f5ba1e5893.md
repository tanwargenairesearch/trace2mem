# Harbor capacity calculations (tool, 50% headroom)

Tool results: 3600 events/s with 1.5 headroom needs 9 replicas (5400 events/s capacity), 466,560,000,000 raw bytes/day if sustained; 400 events/s with 1.5 headroom needs 1 replica, 51,840,000,000 bytes/day. Assumption excludes indexes, replicas, compression, metadata. At 7-day retention, average load implies ~338 GiB raw (assistant arithmetic), exceeding 100 GiB free.
