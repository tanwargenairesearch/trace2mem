# Harbor v1 architecture decision

User confirmed v1: Go, PostgreSQL outbox, EU-only storage, EUR 900/month ceiling, at-least-once delivery with event_id deduplication. Launch moved to 1 December 2026 (was 15 November). Legal approved 7-day raw payload retention (replacing 30-day proposal); metadata stays 90 days. New peak requirement 3600 events/s (was 2400); nine consumers replaces six, conditional on validating DB contention. No Kafka adoption approved.
